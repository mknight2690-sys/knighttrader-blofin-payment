How it works

1

You fund a BloFin account

You open a BloFin account, buy a small amount of crypto with your debit card on Coinbase, and send it to BloFin. That is your trading money.

2

You create a BloFin API key and Nous Portal key

An API key is a long password that lets the app place trades on your behalf without you logging in each time. For Nous Portal, add a one-time ~$5 credit top-up (paid to Nous) to unlock API key creation. The app auto-selects free AI models — no recurring Nous fee unless you manually pick a paid model.

3

You install the app

Download the installer, run it, and let it configure everything for you. No terminal, no Python, no PATH edits.

4

The assistant runs on a 5-minute schedule

Every 5 minutes the assistant checks your account and the market, asks its AI model whether to act, and either does nothing or places a small order. There's never more than 5 minutes where the markets aren't being exploited to your advantage.

5

You keep the kill switch

You can stop the bot anytime from the app, or simply close it. Your money never leaves BloFin.

Questions beginners actually ask

Does this work on a Mac?

Yes. KnightTrader BloFin ships a signed `.dmg` for macOS 11 (Big Sur) and newer on both Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.

I have never bought crypto before. Can I still use this?

Yes. The How-To tab starts with how to open a Coinbase account, buy USDC with your debit card, and send it to BloFin.

Do I need a VPN?

Maybe. Depending on where you live, you may need a VPN to reach the BloFin website.

Is the $5 Nous Portal cost recurring?

No — not from KnightTrader. Nous Portal requires a one-time ~$5 credit top-up (paid directly to Nous) before you can create an API key. After that, the app auto-selects free AI models. You only pay Nous again if you manually switch to a paid model or Nous changes its billing.

Risk: Can I lose money?

Yes. KnightTrader BloFin trades perpetual futures, which are leveraged derivatives. Only fund the BloFin account with money you can afford to lose in full.
